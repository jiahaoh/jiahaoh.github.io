Hello and welcome to my blog!

## About Me

I'm Jiahao Huang, a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical Biology at MIT, working with Prof. Xiao Wang. My research sits at the intersection of computational biology, AI, and spatial genomics.

## Research Focus

My work primarily involves:

- **Spatial Transcriptomics**: Developing tools and pipelines for analyzing spatially resolved gene expression data
- **AI for Science**: Applying machine learning and deep learning to biological problems
- **Production-grade Tooling**: Building robust, reproducible computational workflows

Some of my key projects include:
- **Starfinder**: An end-to-end analysis toolkit for spatial transcriptomics (Nature Protocols 2025)
- **RIBOmap**: Spatially resolved single-cell translatomics (Science 2023)
- **STARmap PLUS**: Integrative in-situ mapping of mouse brain (Nature Neuroscience 2023)

## What to Expect

On this blog, I plan to share:
- Insights from my research journey
- Tutorials on computational biology tools
- Thoughts on AI and its applications in science
- Occasional personal reflections

Feel free to reach out via email or connect with me on LinkedIn or GitHub. I'm always happy to discuss research, collaboration opportunities, or just chat about science!

Thanks for visiting!
